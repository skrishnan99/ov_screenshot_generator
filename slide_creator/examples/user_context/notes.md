This camera is Slave Camera 3 in a four-camera welding cell at our Monterrey plant.
It inspects the TAIL end of stamped rail brackets coming off the press line.

The problem: manual inspectors were missing hairline cracks along the flange and
mis-drilled / missing holes, causing downstream assembly failures and warranty
claims. Missing horn ends also slipped through at a rate of ~2%.

We deployed the OV80i with three models (horn quality, cracks, hole presence) and
integrated pass/fail into the PLC over EtherNet/IP. Total deployment took 3 days
including fixturing.
